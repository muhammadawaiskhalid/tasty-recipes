Beef Burger

A juicy homemade beef burger made with simple and fresh ingredients.

Recipe Information

Preparation Time: 15 minutes

Cooking Time: 15 minutes

Servings: 4

Ingredients

  • 500g ground beef
  • 4 burger buns
  • 4 cheese slices
  • 1 tomato
  • 1 onion
  • Lettuce leaves
  • 2 tablespoons burger sauce
  • Salt to taste
  • Black pepper to taste

Instructions

  1. Season the ground beef with salt and black pepper.
  2. Divide the beef into four portions and shape them into patties.
  3. Heat a pan and cook each patty until properly cooked.
  4. Place a cheese slice on each patty during the final minute of cooking.
  5. Lightly toast the burger buns.
  6. Spread burger sauce on the buns.
  7. Add lettuce, beef patty, tomato, and onion.
  8. Close the burger and serve immediately.

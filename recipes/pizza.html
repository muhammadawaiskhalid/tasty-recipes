Homemade Pizza.

A crispy homemade pizza topped with rich tomato sauce, melted cheese and fresh ingredients for an irresistible family meal.

Prep Time 25 min
Cook Time 20 min
Total Time 45 min
Servings 4
Difficulty Medium
What You'll Need

Ingredients

Step By Step

Instructions

01

Preheat the oven

Preheat the oven to 220°C (425°F). Place your baking tray or pizza pan inside while the oven heats.

02

Shape the dough

Lightly flour your work surface and stretch or roll the pizza dough into your preferred shape and thickness.

03

Add the sauce

Spread the tomato sauce evenly over the dough, leaving a small border around the outside for the crust.

04

Add the toppings

Sprinkle mozzarella over the sauce, then arrange the bell pepper, onion and mushrooms evenly across the pizza.

05

Bake until golden

Transfer the pizza to the hot baking tray and bake until the crust is golden and the cheese is melted and bubbling.

06

Finish and serve

Drizzle lightly with olive oil, sprinkle with oregano and garnish with fresh basil. Slice and serve while hot.

Chef's Tip

Preheat your baking surface.

A hot baking tray helps the bottom of the pizza start cooking immediately, giving the crust a crispier texture.

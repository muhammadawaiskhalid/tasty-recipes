Vegetable Fried Rice.

A quick and satisfying fried rice packed with colorful vegetables, eggs and savory seasoning for an easy everyday meal.

Prep Time 10 min
Cook Time 15 min
Total Time 25 min
Servings 3
Difficulty Easy
What You'll Need

Ingredients

Step By Step

Instructions

01

Prepare the rice

Use cooked rice that has completely cooled. Gently separate any large clumps before cooking.

02

Cook the eggs

Heat a little oil in a large pan or wok. Add the beaten eggs and scramble until just cooked, then set them aside.

03

Cook the vegetables

Add the remaining oil, carrots, peas and bell pepper. Stir-fry until the vegetables are tender but still crisp.

04

Add the rice

Add the cooled rice to the pan and stir-fry over medium-high heat until everything is evenly combined.

05

Season the rice

Add the soy sauce, black pepper and a small amount of salt if needed. Return the scrambled eggs to the pan.

06

Finish and serve

Stir in the spring onions, cook for another minute and serve the fried rice immediately while hot.

Chef's Tip

Use cold cooked rice.

Chilled rice is firmer and contains less surface moisture, helping the grains stay separate instead of becoming soft and sticky while frying.

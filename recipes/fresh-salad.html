Fresh Garden Salad.

A colorful combination of crisp vegetables, fresh greens and a light homemade dressing for a quick and refreshing meal.

Prep Time 15 min
Cook Time 0 min
Total Time 15 min
Servings 2
Difficulty Easy
What You'll Need

Ingredients

Step By Step

Instructions

01

Wash the vegetables

Rinse the lettuce and vegetables thoroughly under clean running water, then dry them before preparing.

02

Prepare the greens

Tear or chop the lettuce into bite-sized pieces and place it into a large salad bowl.

03

Prepare the vegetables

Slice the cucumber, tomatoes, red onion and bell pepper into fresh bite-sized pieces.

04

Combine the salad

Add the prepared vegetables and sweet corn to the bowl with the lettuce and gently combine.

05

Make the dressing

Whisk together the olive oil, lemon juice, salt and black pepper in a small bowl.

06

Toss and serve

Pour the dressing over the salad just before serving. Toss gently until everything is lightly coated.

Chef's Tip

Dress the salad just before serving.

Adding the dressing at the last moment helps the lettuce and vegetables stay crisp, fresh and full of texture.

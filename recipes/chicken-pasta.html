Creamy Chicken Pasta.

Tender chicken, creamy sauce and perfectly cooked pasta come together in a comforting meal that's simple enough for any day.

Prep Time 15 min
Cook Time 25 min
Total Time 40 min
Servings 4
Difficulty Easy
What You'll Need

Ingredients

Step By Step

Instructions

01

Cook the pasta

Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil. Cook the pasta according to the package instructions until al dente, then drain and set aside.

02

Prepare the chicken

Cut the chicken into bite-sized pieces and season with salt and black pepper. Heat olive oil in a large pan over medium-high heat.

03

Cook until golden

Add the chicken to the hot pan and cook until golden on the outside and fully cooked through. Remove temporarily from the pan.

04

Make the creamy sauce

Reduce the heat and add the minced garlic. Cook briefly, then add the cream and Parmesan. Stir until smooth and creamy.

05

Bring everything together

Return the chicken to the pan and add the cooked pasta. Toss everything together until the pasta is evenly coated with the sauce.

06

Serve

Taste and adjust the seasoning if needed. Finish with fresh parsley and additional Parmesan before serving.

Chef's Tip

Save a little pasta water.

If your sauce becomes too thick, add a small amount of reserved pasta water until you reach your preferred consistency.

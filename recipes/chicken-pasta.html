Chicken Pasta

A creamy and delicious chicken pasta perfect for a quick homemade meal.

Recipe Information

Preparation Time: 15 minutes

Cooking Time: 25 minutes

Servings: 4

Ingredients

  • 250g pasta
  • 300g boneless chicken
  • 1 tablespoon cooking oil
  • 2 cloves garlic
  • 1 cup cooking cream
  • 1/2 cup grated cheese
  • Salt to taste
  • Black pepper to taste

Instructions

  1. Boil the pasta according to the package instructions.
  2. Cut the chicken into small pieces.
  3. Heat oil in a pan and cook the chicken until golden.
  4. Add chopped garlic and cook for one minute.
  5. Add the cooking cream and mix well.
  6. Add cheese, salt, and black pepper.
  7. Add the cooked pasta and mix everything together.
  8. Cook for another 2–3 minutes and serve hot.

Fluffy Homemade Pancakes.

Light, soft and golden pancakes made with everyday ingredients for an easy and comforting breakfast.

Prep Time 10 min
Cook Time 15 min
Total Time 25 min
Servings 4
Difficulty Easy
What You'll Need

Ingredients

Step By Step

Instructions

01

Mix the dry ingredients

Add the flour, sugar, baking powder and salt to a large bowl. Whisk until evenly combined.

02

Prepare the wet mixture

In another bowl, whisk the eggs, milk, melted butter and vanilla extract together until smooth.

03

Make the batter

Pour the wet ingredients into the dry ingredients. Stir gently until just combined. A few small lumps are fine.

04

Heat the pan

Place a non-stick pan over medium heat and lightly coat it with butter or oil.

05

Cook the pancakes

Pour a small amount of batter into the pan. Cook until bubbles appear on the surface and the edges begin to set. Flip and cook the other side until golden.

06

Serve warm

Stack the pancakes and serve while warm with fruit, honey, maple syrup or your favorite toppings.

Chef's Tip

Don't overmix the batter.

Mix only until the ingredients come together. Overmixing can make pancakes dense instead of light and fluffy.

Pancakes

Soft and fluffy homemade pancakes perfect for breakfast.

Recipe Information

Preparation Time: 10 minutes

Cooking Time: 15 minutes

Servings: 4

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 tablespoon baking powder
  • 2 tablespoons sugar
  • 1/4 teaspoon salt
  • 1 egg
  • 1 1/4 cups milk
  • 2 tablespoons melted butter
  • Honey or syrup for serving

Instructions

  1. Mix the flour, baking powder, sugar, and salt in a bowl.
  2. In another bowl, whisk the egg, milk, and melted butter.
  3. Add the wet ingredients to the dry ingredients.
  4. Mix gently until combined.
  5. Heat a lightly greased pan over medium heat.
  6. Pour a small amount of batter into the pan.
  7. Cook until bubbles appear, then flip the pancake.
  8. Cook the other side until golden.
  9. Serve warm with honey or syrup.

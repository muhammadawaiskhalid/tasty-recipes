Rich Chocolate Cake.

A soft, moist and deeply chocolatey homemade cake that's perfect for celebrations, desserts or whenever you're craving something sweet.

Prep Time 20 min
Bake Time 35 min
Total Time 55 min
Servings 8
Difficulty Medium
What You'll Need

Ingredients

Step By Step

Instructions

01

Prepare the oven

Preheat the oven to 180°C (350°F). Lightly grease a cake pan and line the bottom with baking paper.

02

Combine dry ingredients

Add the flour, sugar, cocoa powder, baking powder and baking soda to a large mixing bowl and whisk together.

03

Add wet ingredients

Add the eggs, milk, vegetable oil and vanilla extract. Mix until the batter becomes smooth and evenly combined.

04

Fill the cake pan

Pour the chocolate batter into the prepared pan and gently level the surface with a spatula.

05

Bake the cake

Bake until the center is set and a toothpick inserted into the middle comes out with only a few moist crumbs.

06

Cool and decorate

Allow the cake to cool completely before adding chocolate frosting. Slice and serve.

Baker's Tip

Don't overbake the cake.

Check the cake near the end of the baking time. Removing it once it is just cooked through helps keep the inside soft and moist.
